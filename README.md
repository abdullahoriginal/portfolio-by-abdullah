# Abdullah's Portfolio Website

> **Domain:** [builtbyabdullah.me](https://builtbyabdullah.me) | **Hosting:** Vercel | **Framework:** Next.js (App Router)

A dark-theme portfolio website engineered for **Mirza Abdullah Adil** — full-stack e-commerce specialist and graphic designer.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Design System

- **Base Background:** `#0a0a0a` (Deep Black)
- **Card Background:** `#0f0f1a` (Almost Black)
- **Primary Accent:** `#e63946` (Bold Deep Red)
- **Secondary Accent:** `#3a3a3a` (Subtle Borders & Dividers)
- **Text Primary:** `#ffffff` (Pure White)
- **Text Secondary:** `#b0b0b0` (Light Gray)
- **Typography:** DM Serif Display (Headings) & DM Sans (Body / Navigation)

---

## 🚀 Sections Included

1. **Fixed Header / Navigation:** Logo, section anchors, "Let's Talk" CTA, and responsive mobile drawer.
2. **Hero:** Signature headline, e-commerce value proposition, primary CTAs, and 3-pillar credibility ribbon.
3. **Key Metrics / Social Proof:** 6 verified metric cards (130,000+ SAR Noon GMV, 136% ROI, +30% CTR, 10+ SKUs, 3+ Years, 3.52 CGPA).
4. **Services / What I Do:** 6 specialized capability cards with bullet deliverables and keyword taxonomy.
5. **Featured Case Studies:**
   - *Case Study 1:* 136% ROI Noon Marketplace Growth (11K -> 130K+ SAR)
   - *Case Study 2:* 30%+ Conversion Lift Fonethics Amazon Relaunch
   - *Case Study 3:* Creative Showcase with category filter tabs and inspection modal
   - *Case Study 4:* ChurnSense (FYP) with **Live Interactive ML Simulator Widget**
6. **Experience:** Vertical timeline detailing all 6 career milestones with bullet points and skill pills.
7. **Tech Stack & Tools:** Categorized toolkits (Languages, E-Commerce, Design, Automation/Data Science).
8. **About:** Deep personal bio, academic achievements, languages, and hobbies.
9. **Contact:** Interactive brief submission form + direct email, phone/WhatsApp, and social links.
10. **Footer:** Quick links, back-to-top button, and copyright branding.
